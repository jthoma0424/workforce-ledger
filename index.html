Labor, freight and overhead are not included.

Uploads and conversion tables for All Truss job costing. Job results are on the Jobs tab.
Add jobs and invoices
Upload the QuickBooks invoices and the Material Summary reports for jobs that have been invoiced. Either one can be uploaded on its own — jobs and invoices are matched by job number (the Estimate No. on the QuickBooks invoice = the Transaction # on the Material Summary). An invoice with no matching Estimate No. is also matched when its invoice number equals the Structure Number on a Material Summary. The job category on the Material Summary decides the plates: Residential and Commercial are costed with Mitek plates, Agriculture with Eagle plates. Uploading the same job or invoice again replaces it.
QuickBooks product costs (monthly)
Upload the QuickBooks Inventory Valuation Summary for the month. Each product's cost is Asset Value ÷ Qty. A job is costed with the file for the month it was invoiced. Shorts are converted to the stocked length they are cut from (see below). Plates are matched by size to the Mitek or Eagle plates in this file, depending on the job category (Residential and Commercial use Mitek; Agriculture uses Eagle, with the 18 gauge HS plates matched to Eagle HS). Anything not found keeps the truss software's cost and is flagged.
Shorts conversion
One line per length: short = stocked. A short piece is costed as its share of the stocked piece (qty × short length ÷ stocked length), for example 14 pieces of 7-00-00 cost the same as 7 pieces of 14-00-00. If that stocked length isn’t carried (no QuickBooks cost), the short is fitted into the next size up that is carried (as many short pieces per stocked piece as fit, so 7 ft into 8 ft is one for one and 3 ft into 8 ft is two per piece), and the job is charged the full stocked piece; the leftover length is listed under Fit-up offcuts. Remove a line to stop converting that length.
Material substitutions
One line per product: design product = stocked product. The job is costed as the stocked product of the same length, one piece for one piece. For example, 2400F material is costed as DSS of the same size. Any “2x? SP 2400F…” product not listed here is also treated as the DSS of that size.
Plate name matches
Plates are matched to QuickBooks by size automatically (Residential and Commercial → Mitek, Agriculture → Eagle). If a plate on the Material Summary is named differently in QuickBooks, add one line: Eagle: MT20 5x6 = 5x6 HS (software name = the QuickBooks item name). Start the line with Eagle: or Mitek: to limit it to that plate type. Plates still using the software price are listed under “Products with no QuickBooks cost” on the Jobs tab.
