Privacy Policy

Workforce Ledger, an internal application of Workforce Strategies LLC and its affiliated companies. Last updated: November 25, 2026.

Who uses this app

Workforce Ledger is a private, internal business application. It is used only by authorized owners and staff of Workforce Strategies LLC and its affiliated companies. It is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

What we access from QuickBooks Online

When an authorized administrator connects a QuickBooks Online company, the app reads and creates accounting data for that company only: customers, invoices, items, classes, locations, terms, bank account balances, and balance sheet reports. It creates invoices only when an administrator reviews and approves them.

How the data is stored and protected

Connection credentials (access and refresh tokens) are stored on the server in a restricted database table that the website cannot read. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to the app requires a signed-in administrator account. We do not sell, rent, or share QuickBooks data with third parties, and we use it only to run our own bookkeeping, invoicing, and reporting.

Retention and disconnecting

An administrator can disconnect any QuickBooks company at any time from the app's Settings page, which deletes the stored credentials and revokes the app's access at Intuit. Questions about data deletion can be directed to the Workforce Strategies LLC account administrator.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above shows the latest revision.
